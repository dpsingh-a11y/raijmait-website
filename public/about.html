Rajmait was founded by former CTOs and infrastructure leads who were tired of consulting firms that sold hours instead of outcomes.

Our Story

From a two-person team to a 40-consultant practice

Rajmait started in 2014 when our founders left enterprise engineering roles to help startups avoid the same infrastructure mistakes they'd spent years fixing. What began as freelance cloud migrations grew into a full-service IT consultancy trusted by fintech, healthcare, and logistics companies across three continents.

Today we combine deep technical execution with the strategic thinking of a boardroom advisor — because good IT decisions are business decisions.

Our Mission

Give growing companies enterprise-grade IT capability without the enterprise-grade headcount, cost, or bureaucracy.

Our Vision

Be the first call any CTO makes when infrastructure, security, or scale becomes a business risk.
